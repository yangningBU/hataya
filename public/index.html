What can I look up?

  • Nouns: masculine or feminine, plus the plural
  • Verbs: the root, the binyan and every form (past, present, future, imperative)
  • Adjectives: masculine and feminine, singular and plural

Type any form, for example a conjugated verb like הלכתי, and Hataya finds the dictionary word.

Common words work offline. For anything else, Hataya asks AI when you're online.

Forms follow standard vocalization and the Academy of the Hebrew Language's plain-spelling rules. AI answers can contain mistakes.
